OffLoad Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

OffLoad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

OffLoad does not transmit personal journal data to external servers.

All journal entries and app data are stored locally on your device using SQLite.

No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertisements, and no account is required.

The app does not connect to the internet.
