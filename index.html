OffLoad Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

OffLoad is designed as a local-first journaling application focused on privacy and simplicity.

This Privacy Policy explains how OffLoad handles your information, device permissions, and locally stored content.

1. Information Handling

OffLoad does not require an account.

OffLoad does not collect personal information, create user profiles, or store journal data on developer-controlled servers.

Journal entries, audio recordings, settings, and related app data are stored locally on your device using on-device storage technologies such as SQLite and local file storage.

2. Microphone & Audio Recording

OffLoad requests microphone access to allow users to:

  • Record voice journal entries
  • Use speech-to-text transcription features
  • Play back recorded audio entries

Audio recordings are stored locally on your device and may remain associated with journal entries until deleted by you.

If you edit an entry and create additional recordings, multiple recordings may remain associated with that entry unless removed.

You may delete entries and associated recordings from within the app.

You can also revoke microphone permission at any time through your device settings.

3. Speech Recognition

OffLoad uses device and operating-system speech recognition services to convert speech into text.

On supported Android 14+ devices, OffLoad may use on-device speech recognition features that support multilingual and bilingual language detection.

Depending on your device configuration, installed language models, and operating system behavior, speech recognition processing may be handled either:

  • entirely on-device, or
  • by speech recognition services provided by your device platform provider (such as Google speech services on Android).

OffLoad itself does not upload, store, or process journal entries or audio recordings on developer-controlled servers.

4. Local Storage

OffLoad stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Journal entries
  • Audio recordings
  • App settings and preferences
  • Mood selections and metadata
  • Archived entries

Archived entries remain stored locally on your device until deleted by you.

Deleting an entry removes the associated locally stored content from the app's storage.

5. Notifications

OffLoad may use local notifications to provide journaling reminders.

These notifications are scheduled and stored locally on your device.

OffLoad does not use remote push notifications or notification servers.

6. Internet Connectivity

OffLoad is designed to function primarily offline.

The app does not upload journal entries or audio recordings to developer-controlled servers.

However, certain operating-system services or platform-level features, such as speech recognition services or app update infrastructure, may use internet connectivity depending on your device and system configuration.

7. Third-Party Services

OffLoad currently does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Crash reporting services
  • User tracking technologies

Future versions of the app may introduce optional diagnostic or crash reporting tools to improve app stability. If this occurs, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Security

Because OffLoad stores data locally on your device, the security of your information also depends on your device security practices, such as screen locks, encryption, and device access controls.

9. Children's Privacy

OffLoad is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
